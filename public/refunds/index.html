Our money-back guarantee

If a certification is not right for you, tell us within 14 days of enrolling and we will refund what you paid, as long as you have not completed the course or downloaded the certificate.

How to ask

Email info@thepeerscertification.com from the address you enrolled with and tell us which certification you would like refunded. You do not need to give a reason.

What happens next

We refund the card you paid with through our payment provider, Stripe. Banks usually take 5 to 10 working days to show it. Your access to that certification ends, and any certificate issued for it is withdrawn and will no longer verify.

Limits

A refund is not available once you have completed every module or downloaded your certificate, or after 14 days from enrolling.

Your legal rights

This guarantee is in addition to any rights you have under consumer law where you live.
