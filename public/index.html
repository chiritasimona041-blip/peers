What a coach does

You do not give advice. You ask better questions.

A coach helps someone work out what they want, what is in the way and what they will do about it. The answers are theirs. The skill is yours.

01

Listen

Properly, without waiting for your turn to speak. Most people are rarely listened to like this.

02

Ask

The question that makes someone stop, think and see their situation differently.

03

Hold them to it

Help them choose a next step, then be the person who asks how it went.

Questions

Questions people ask.

Do I need experience to become a coach?

No. Every certification starts from first principles. Most people who enrol have never coached professionally.

What does a coach actually do?

A coach helps someone work out what they want, what is in the way and what they will do next, through listening, questioning and accountability. Coaching is not therapy and not advice.

Can I study while working full time?

Yes. Everything is online and self-paced, with 12 months of access, so you can study in evenings, at weekends or in short sessions.

Can I study on my phone?

Yes. The modules work on phones, tablets and laptops.

What happens after I pass?

Your certificate is issued straight away with its own number. It stays in your account, and anyone can confirm it on our register.

Can I take more than one certification?

Yes. Many coaches start with a foundation certification and add a specialism. Each one is examined and certified separately.

How do I get access after paying?

Your login details are emailed as soon as payment goes through. Choose a password and start the first module straight away.

How long does a certification take?

It is self-paced. At a few hours a week you can finish in a few months, and you have 12 months of access.

How is the exam marked?

The final exam is multiple choice and marked instantly. You need 80% to pass and can retake it.

Who recognises the certificate?

Your certificate is issued by The Peers Certification. Coaching is not a regulated profession, so you do not need a licence to practise; what clients look for is training they can check, and every certificate carries a number anyone can verify on our site.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. You are covered by our 14-day money-back guarantee, as long as you have not completed the course or downloaded the certificate.
